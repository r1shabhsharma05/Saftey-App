What SafeLife is

SafeLife is a personal-safety app. It lets you send SOS alerts to emergency contacts and guardians you choose, share live journey tracking with them, and verify guardians by SMS code. The app is provided by Rishabh Sharma.

Important: SafeLife is not a substitute for emergency services. In an emergency, always call your local emergency number first (for example 112 in India). SafeLife alerts your personal contacts; it does not contact police, ambulance, or any emergency service, and it does not guarantee that any contact will see or act on an alert. SOS delivery depends on your phone, network coverage, and the messaging services we use, and can fail.

Your account

  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials safe and for everything done through your account.
  • You must give accurate contact details. SOS alerts go to the numbers you provide — wrong numbers mean the wrong people are alerted, or nobody is.
  • Only add emergency contacts and guardians who have agreed to receive your alerts.
  • Users under 18 need verifiable parental consent (see our Privacy Policy).
