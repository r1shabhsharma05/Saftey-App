Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

SafeLife is a personal-safety app: it sends SOS alerts to your emergency contacts and guardians, tracks journeys you choose to share, and verifies guardians by SMS code. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, and how you can delete it. If you do not agree with it, do not use the app.

Data we collect

  • Account: name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and profile photo. Your address is not collected.
  • Location: precise live location during an SOS alert and during a journey you start, including the journey trail.
  • Emergency contacts and guardians: their names and phone numbers, which you provide.
  • SOS alerts: alert details, status, and battery level at the time of the alert.
  • Voice: voice audio up to 30 seconds when you use voice features (transcription and speech synthesis).
  • Device: device identifiers and push-notification tokens (when push is enabled).

Why we collect it

  • To send SOS alerts to your guardians and emergency contacts.
  • To track journeys you start, so guardians can follow them and be alerted if you deviate or are overdue.
  • To verify guardians by SMS code before they can receive your alerts.
  • To enforce the 13-and-over age limit: your date of birth is checked at sign-up and at sign-in, and is not shared with anyone.
  • To operate your account and keep the service working.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication. Your rows are protected by owner-only row-level security, so only your account can read them; your profile photo is stored in a private bucket and served through time-limited signed URLs.
  • Telegram — delivers SOS alerts to guardians who use Telegram.
  • httpSMS — sends verification codes and SOS alerts by SMS.
  • Gnani Vachana — transcribes voice audio (speech-to-text) and generates spoken alerts (text-to-speech). Only the audio or text you submit for that feature is sent.
  • Mapbox, Overpass, and OSRM — provide maps, nearby safe places, and journey routes. Your location is sent to them only to fetch map data for you.
  • Google Maps — SOS messages include a Google Maps link to your location, sent only to your chosen contacts and guardians.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — delivers push notifications about SOS alerts.

How data is protected

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access to your stored data is limited to your own account through Supabase row-level security. Your profile photo is kept in a private storage bucket and is only accessible through signed URLs that expire.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data, contacts, journeys, and alert records while your account exists, so the service can work for you. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. Deleting your account removes your profile, emergency contacts, guardians, journeys, SOS alerts, and profile photo from our systems.

What deletion cannot undo: copies already sent to other people or services cannot be recalled. SMS and Telegram messages already delivered to your guardians stay with them. Voice audio and transcripts sent to Gnani are held under Gnani’s own retention policy, which we do not control. What Gnani publishes: customer voice data is not used for model training without explicit agreement (see Gnani’s FAQ at gnani.ai), and Gnani’s privacy policy states personal information is kept only as long as required for the purpose or by law, whichever is later — no fixed retention period for voice data is published. You can ask Gnani to delete your data or stop using it by emailing hello@gnani.ai (see Gnani’s privacy policy at voicebiometrics.ai/privacy-policy). Ask us (below) and we will tell you what we sent and when.

Children

SafeLife is only for people aged 13 and over. We enforce this at sign-up and at sign-in: every account must have a date of birth on record, and accounts under 13 cannot be created or used. Your date of birth is collected at sign-up, stored in Supabase Auth (your account record — not in your profile), and is not shared with anyone. There is no parental-consent flow, because under-13s are not admitted at all. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
